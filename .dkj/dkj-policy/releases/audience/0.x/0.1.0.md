# Release notes v0.1.0 🚀

**Date:** 2026-10-08\
**Type:** Minor\
**For whom:** consumers of this product, and colleagues in the organisation -- one section each

First release: the EXP-per-meso calculator for Classic World

## What changed

This is the first numbered version of MesoWise. It answers one question at every level-up: **how many mesos will
your next level cost, and how do you make that cheaper?**

**Do you need to do anything?** Only one thing. If you used the app before October 4, your saved list of training
spots is gone. Pick the mob you hunt on the Monster card instead. Everything else carries over.

### See what your next level costs

Pick the mob you hunt and the app shows what your level costs in mesos right away: potions, stars or arrows, and
travel. The **Total cost** card compares your setup with the cheapest one the app can find and shows the difference
per kind of cost. One tap on **Overnemen** switches you to the cheapest setup, and **Ongedaan maken** puts everything
back.

### Know what to buy, and what to skip

The **Equip** card tells you which weapon or armor piece pays for itself over the coming levels and which does not.
It shows the shop price and what the piece costs you this level. It covers weapons, armor, overalls versus top and
pants, shields, gloves, capes and earrings. It only offers items your character can actually wear. When two items
save the same, it picks the cheaper one.

### Place your skill point where it saves the most

The **Skillpoints** card says which skill to raise and why, weighed over your next five levels rather than this one
alone. Defensive skills and recovery skills count too, so a Warrior or Magician can see what a point in recovery saves
on potions.

### Level up in one tap

**Level up** keeps your stats and equipment and updates level, Max HP and accuracy for you. The cards show how many AP
and SP you still have to place. **Auto assign** fills in your AP so every item your level allows fits. Tapped by
mistake? **Back** returns you to the level you came from.

### Every job and every level

Thief (claw or dagger), Warrior, Magician and Bowman each get advice for their own route. Below level 10 you are
calculated as the Beginner you still are, with the weapon in your hand and no ammo to pay for.

### On your phone, also offline

Add the app to your home screen and it opens without signal. The menu also offers a single-file download that runs in a
browser on a PC without internet. Popups use almost the full phone width, and the page behind a popup no longer scrolls
along. A question mark next to Accuracy and Evasion shows how the number is built, with your own stats.

The game data (monsters, items, EXP table) comes from NiaMeowDB (meowdb.com), one item at a time, each with its source.

## What it is worth

- **The level-up question no longer needs a spreadsheet or a guess.** Before this release, choosing a training spot,
  an upgrade or a skill point meant doing the arithmetic by hand or trusting a hunch. The app now answers all three in
  one screen, for every job, from level 1.
- **The number is guarded.** 1,946 automated tests ran green on the release commit, most of them on the calculation.
  A wrong cost is the one defect that matters here, and every change to the calculation passes those tests before it
  lands.
- **Risk kept out of a public repo.** Game data is taken item by item with its source, never as a whole table, which
  keeps it within NiaMeowDB's reuse policy. No sprites, icons or brand material from Nexon are in it.
- **What this release cost to cut:** clock started 08:43:45. The cut itself (lint gate + 61 test files, commit and tag)
  took 1m 24s, to 08:45:09. A concurrent push to `main` (#244 to #249) had to be merged in afterwards. That took 1m 10s
  more, to 08:46:19, and leaves those five changes pending for the next release. Subtotal frozen here: **2m 34s**.
  Writing this note, its gate run, the GitHub Release and the last attachment brought the total to **3m 44s**
  (08:47:29).
